## 2026-10-08

The Study 2 pipeline is built to its registration, `Docs/Preregistration/AsPredicted/Study2_AsPredicted.md`, which was registered on OSF (the Open Science Framework) on 8 October 2026 as part of one Open-Ended Registration of the five studies, project https://osf.io/u3d69. It was run blind only. No outcome has been modelled or summarised on the real labels in this pipeline.

- `Study2_01_Quant.Rmd` reads the Values and Labels exports and the survey file through the shared helpers and applies the five registered exclusion steps in order (275 records, then 275, 244, 235, 235 and 235). It stops unless the flow and every count the registration states hold: 122 Human and 113 AI participants, all 235 right on the task question, every participant shown all ten source images (five with a figure, five without), 228 participants with 90 ratings and 7 with 87 who lack only the trial 1FigureMin, 21,129 ratings and as many justifications, 470 of each on the two six-point items, and 20,659 of each analysed. The shared importer leaves out the Trash block, and with it the duplicate 1FigureMaxCreat (QID28). Each rating is scored by its position on the answer scale, and the two six-point transformativeness items are dropped with their justifications. The file fits the registered model on each measure, `rating ~ source * modification * image_kind + (1 | id) + (1 | image)`, with sum-to-zero contrasts, REML and the bobyqa optimizer. It runs the four registered tests per measure as contrasts of estimated marginal means, averaged equally over the other factors, with Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, and corrects the twelve by Holm. It also gives the equivalence test of the Human minus AI difference at plus or minus 0.224, the reading of H1, H2 and H3 on each measure, the cumulative-link check of tests 1, 2 and 4, the exploratory Human minus AI contrast at each modification level, the status of every fit and a run manifest.
- `Study2_02_Text.ipynb` measures the 20,659 analysed justifications with the shared module on a blind slice built from four columns of the long data file (participant, second work, question, text). The second work is the stimulus, and its source image, image kind and modification level are read off its name. The notebook checks that the module's rules, written out for Study 1, equal Study 1's deposited `tables/text_rules.txt` and its SHA-256, and that Study 2's rules text differs from Study 1's only in the title and response-count lines. It was run in place with the `python312` kernel.
- `Study2_03_Integration.Rmd` checks each input against the run manifest that wrote it, joins the measures to the ratings and the source condition, and runs the two registered text checks on the creativity justifications through `shared/R/text_checks.R`, with the second work as the stimulus. It then runs the exploratory items the registration names: the same two checks on the transformativeness and essence justifications, tone and concreteness, topics, and the words the rating prediction leans on most.
- `README.md` and this file.

Where the registration leaves a choice open, the code takes these readings.

- Tests 1 and 2 are two-sided t tests. H1 holds on a measure only when both estimates are positive and both tests survive Holm.
- Test 3 enters the Holm family through the larger of its two one-sided p-values, and its equivalence verdict rests on the Holm-corrected p-value.
- The cumulative-link check computes tests 1, 2 and 4 as the same contrasts on the model's underlying scale, each with a Wald z test. Holm corrects them in a family of twelve that keeps the linear model's three equivalence p-values for test 3, which the check does not cover.

- The exploratory word-overlap check on transformativeness and essence change runs one measure at a time, so neither is corrected with the other.

## 2026-10-08: the hypotheses over the three measures, and the word weights of the source model

Two commitments of the registration were carried out only in part. These changes to the source files complete them.

- `Study2_01_Quant.Rmd`, section 5.4. H1 says that ratings rise on all three measures, and H2 that the penalty is small or absent on each. The file read both on each measure only. It now also reads each over the three measures, where it holds only when it holds on every measure. H3 asks a question of each measure and has no such reading. `study2_hypothesis_readings.csv` therefore has eleven rows, nine by measure and two over all three.
- `Study2_03_Integration.Rmd`, section 4.4. The registration names as exploratory the words the first text check leans on most, and that check predicts both the rating and the source condition. The file gave the words for the rating only. It now fits a second model on each measure, as Study 1 does: a lasso logistic regression of the source condition (AI against Human) on the word counts. A logistic regression models an outcome with two values, and the lasso penalty sets most word weights to exactly zero. The rating sits in the model with no penalty, so a word keeps a weight only if it tells the conditions apart beyond what the rating does. The penalty is chosen by the binomial deviance of the held-out conditions, minus twice their log-likelihood, where the rating model uses the squared error. The `target` column of `study2_text_prediction_weights.csv` says which model each row comes from, `rating` or `arm`, and the fit log has one lasso row for each measure and model.
- `README.md` describes both tables as they now stand.

After these changes `Study2_01_Quant.Rmd` was knitted again in blind mode, with `UNBLIND_STUDY2` unset and the report sent to `blind_run/`. It reproduced the registered exclusion flow and every registered count. All three registered linear mixed models and all three cumulative-link models converged, none singular. The hypothesis table has its eleven rows, the report opens with the blind sentence, and the run manifest lists all 17 outputs as written by the run.

`Study2_03_Integration.Rmd` was then started again in blind mode and did not reach its end. It matched its inputs to their manifests and wrote the join check, the word-count check and the two registered checks on creativity. Its run manifest and the tables of the same two checks on transformativeness and essence change are not on disk. The tone, topic and word-weight tables and the fit log in `tables/blind_run/`, and the integration report in `blind_run/`, come from the blind run made before these changes, so the word-weight table there holds the rating model only. The integration file has not run to its end since section 4.4 changed, in either mode.

The blind runs ended there. The real-label run that followed is recorded in the next entry.

## 2026-10-08: real-label run

- The five studies were registered on OSF on 8 October 2026 (Open-Ended Registration, project https://osf.io/u3d69). `Study2_01_Quant.Rmd` and `Study2_03_Integration.Rmd` were then knitted with `UNBLIND_STUDY2=TRUE`. Every registered count matched and every registered linear mixed model converged.
- A read-only check traced every commitment of the registration to the code. All are implemented.
- One repair followed the first real-label run. `Study2_03_Integration.Rmd`, section 4.1: the word-overlap rows for transformativeness and essence change, which the registration names as exploratory, carried the role "registered comparison", a Holm p-value and a verdict. They are now labelled "exploratory comparison" with no corrected p-value and no verdict, and the file was knitted again. No registered result changed.
- The cumulative-link check on creativity has no usable fit. The optimizer finished (code 0), but ordinal's inner update of the random effects stopped with a largest gradient of 0.000017 against its tolerance of 0.000001, and the registered convergence rule counts any such warning as non-convergence. More inner iterations, a longer line search and the sparse-matrix option all end at the same point; the ucminf optimizer cannot be used for this model. The check is therefore reported as not completed for creativity. Fitted outside the rule, as description only, its three contrasts on the underlying scale are: Slight minus None 3.59 (z = 34.8), Dramatic minus Slight 3.78 (z = 47.9), and test 4 +0.26 (z = 1.31, p = .19). These agree with the linear model's verdicts on tests 1 and 2 and with its non-significant test 4, though test 4 changes sign on the ordinal scale, as it does on the other two measures, where ratings at None sit near the floor of the scale.

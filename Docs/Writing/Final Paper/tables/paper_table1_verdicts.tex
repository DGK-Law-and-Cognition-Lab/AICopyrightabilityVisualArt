{\raggedright\tiny \textbf{Studies} (analyzed participants; collection): 1 (\SOneN; 25 February to 3 March 2025); 2 (\STwoN; 23 November to 5 December 2024); 3 (\SThreeN; 4 to 9 March 2025); 4 (\SFourN; 27 January to 2 February 2025); 5 (\SFiveN; 21 October to 2 November 2025). \textbf{Registered} means written into a registration, not fixed before collection. All five were filed on OSF on 8 October 2026, after collection: Study~1's after its analyses had been run, the others before the analyses reported here. \textbf{Seen before registration}: April 2025 \emph{t}-tests (Study~1); analyses of variance run by February 2025 and reported at CogSci 2025 (Studies 2 and 4) or run in April 2025 (Study~3); a September 2025 talk on Studies 1, 3 and 4 (`talk'). For Study~5 no rating had been summarized by condition, and both bounds were fixed before any Study~5 model was fitted. \textbf{Status}. \emph{Confirmatory}: decided by the registration's rule, usually after Holm's correction, which raises each $p$-value in a set of tests so that the chance of any false positive in the set stays at .05. \emph{Secondary}: outside that set, and uncorrected unless stated. \emph{Own rule}: Study~1's H3, a significance test read with an equivalence test; its power on creativity was \SOneHThreeCreatPower, so that failed equivalence test is uninformative. An \textbf{equivalence test} asks whether an effect is shown to lie inside a bound. It passes when the 90\% confidence interval lies wholly inside, which means small, not zero. An $F$ test asks whether a factor changes the mean rating. Bounds in points on 1-to-5 scales: $\pm$\SOneHThreeBound{} (Study~1's H3); $\pm$\SourceBound, half of Study~1's creativity penalty of \SOneHOneCreatDiffPrecise{} (label effects and Study~3's gap); $\pm$\SFiveEditTimeBound, the size of Study~3's creativity time effect (editing hours). \textbf{Checks}. Ordinal refits, which treat the answers as ordered categories, agreed with every verdict they re-tested in Studies 2 to 5, except Study~2's creativity refit, which did not converge (not completed). Study~5's analysis with items rescaled from 0 to 1 gave the same verdicts. Study~3's required refit with effects varying by person and image was singular (a variance at the edge of its range), so that requirement was waived. Study~1's H4 needs both ratings; its between-person test survives in the Holm set, which does not mean H4 passed. Flagged: Study~4's \SFourMachineMadeN{} Human-label participants who wrote that a machine made the work. \textbf{Text checks} on the explanations, each written after its rating. Word counts recover the rating in every study: they predict it for people left out of the fit better than a shuffled baseline. They recover the label in Study~1, for Study~3's effort and, barely, in Study~5, and are not shown to elsewhere. They add to the rating in telling the labels apart in Studies 1 and 2 and are inconclusive in Studies 3 to 5. Greater own-word reuse under the AI label is supported for Study~1's effort only.\par}

A registration is a dated record of a study's hypotheses and analysis plan. All five studies were registered on the Open Science Framework (OSF), an online archive for study plans, on 8 October 2026, after their data had been collected; these registrations have nothing to do with copyright registration. Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-verdicts} lists every registered test of the five studies with its status, its verdict and what had been seen before its registration, and Section~\ref{sec:suppA-registrations} defines each status. Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp-labeleffects} gives the AI label's effect on ratings of second works, study by study, measure by measure and level by level, with the interval and the tests behind each verdict.
